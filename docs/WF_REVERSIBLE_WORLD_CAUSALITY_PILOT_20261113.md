# Reversible World Causality — first real-source pilot (2026-11-13)

Status: **SHADOW / world-logic comprehension NOT verified**. Source names and these counts are permitted for a public engineering report; copyrighted originals, named private fact ledgers, and original spans remain private and are NOT in Git.

## Purpose

Establish separately:
1. **Mechanically reversible evidence representation**: byte-identical original restoration, anchored spans, signed chronology/epistemic records and derivation-traced numeric values.
2. **Semantically reversible world causality**: reconstructing constraints, irrational changes and independent off-screen processes across changed names/worlds/character traits, judged on fresh original held-out events.

(1) is a necessary but **not** sufficient condition for (2). No literal story plot may be smuggled into the testing feature labels.

The source-wide raw line indexes cover every byte, including CRLF vs LF, blank lines, non-narrative prefaces, incidental NPCs, chapter heading candidates and outside-POV passages. SHA256 of each original and each line is stored in the **private** audit output. The candidate-heading counts do not prove that every chapter has been correctly delimited; different editions/formatting need chapter sequence review.

For both entire originals, `codec.py pack -> verify -> recover` returned exactly the input bytes. All 41 private anchors located real byte-exact text; the small 20 proposed fact observations and 5 causal links remain **unreviewed hypotheses or tentative interpretations**, not verified world truth. Before completing these pilots, three incorrectly quoted candidate evidence strings were rejected by the exact-anchor checks and repaired from the source. The anonymous projection contained no test anchor surface strings. A one-byte corrupted anchor was rejected.

**No numerical world-logic weights were learned.** New numeric guards explicitly reject a claimed weight without a matching per-parameter source-literal or exact-arithmetic provenance trace. The synthetic decimal examples `-0.0129` and `0.145` check lossless numeric transport and calculation only; do not interpret them as measured literary effects.

## Source-free tests

The private modern originals are NOT CI inputs. Eleven synthetic-only Python unit tests in `tests/test_wf_reversible_world_causality.py` and `tests/test_wf_reversible_world_source_index.py` passed on the feature-branch GitHub Actions isolated workflow. These verify strict UTF-8/GB18030/BOM handling, exact recovery, ungrounded/altered decimals rejected, time and anchor contradictions, name-relabeled graph representation, source-tamper rejection and full preface/body byte coverage. An earlier failed CI run during a deliberate intermediate schema tightening was preserved; the subsequent runs with updated tests passed.

## Hard next gate before promotion

Use the existing `XuehaiStore` as the single literary source, attach private byte-evidence observations, and review each chapter's time/space/actor events including off-screen and incidental entities. Keep **telling sequence ≠ story time**. Freeze source-disjoint heldouts and truly different-world/trait interventions **before** model calls. Compare predicted abstract constraints, local effects and explanations to independently reviewed unseen original events; count omitted consequences and hallucinated links as errors, permit `UNKNOWN` and genuine coincidence. Do not fit on holdouts, and do not force changed personalities to repeat original outcomes. A single inaccurate parameter that loses its inverse invalidates that candidate; preserve the earliest failure and original negative evidence.
